Welcome to The Glovebox. Before buying a bed cover, write down the manufacturer's size name for your bed and the inside length you read off a tape, and keep the two notes separate.

Two ideas for comparing

1

One quick measurement to get your size right

Park and secure your truck, then close the tailgate. Measure straight from the inside face of the bed front wall to the inside face of the closed tailgate. Write down that tape reading and the factory size label separately.

2

Check the exact cover's fitment guide

Open the fitment guide for the exact cover you are considering and compare both notes against it.
