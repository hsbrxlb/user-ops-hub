Buying & fitment

What information should I have before ordering an accessory?

Record the vehicle's year, make, model, and exact configuration. For steps, include cab and existing side hardware. For a cover, include bed length, tailgate, rails, and installed cargo accessories. For liners, include cab, seating, flooring, and rear storage. Save the selected product option as well as the page link.

What if the vehicle selector and product description disagree?

Keep the item unconfirmed. Send support the vehicle details, selected option, and the two conflicting statements. Request the correct application for that part number before ordering. Do not choose whichever statement gives the broader fitment range.

Does the year range in a product URL determine fitment?

No. A URL can retain an older product name after the page changes. Read the selected option and written application. If those disagree, request clarification for the exact part. Preserve body-version and equipment exclusions even when the headline is broad.

Are SuperCab and SuperCrew interchangeable when buying accessories?

Treat them as separate cab designations. Ford identifies them separately, and accessory applications may specify one. Use the exact cab in the listing; door count and a similar-looking vehicle photo do not prove that brackets or interior liners are interchangeable.

Measuring

Where should I measure a truck bed for a cover?

For a basic inside-bed length, close the tailgate and measure between the inside bulkhead face and inside tailgate face. Record inches and photograph both endpoints, noting any liner the tape touches. Follow the selected cover's instructions for any additional measurements. This reading alone does not establish fitment.

Why does my tape reading differ from the advertised bed-size name?

A nominal bed name may differ from the inside dimension. Keep both values instead of rounding the tape reading to match a listing. Also distinguish decimal feet from feet and inches. Ask the seller to resolve a material mismatch before choosing the cover.

Which running-board measurements matter for easier entry?

Compare installed tread height, usable tread depth and width, and its position beneath the door. Ask where the height measurement starts and which tire and suspension setup it describes. Overall length or shipping-box dimensions do not tell you how comfortably you can step into the cab.

Installation & safety

Can I put all-weather mats on top of my existing mats?

No. Remove the existing removable mats. Stacking can reduce pedal clearance and leave a mat unsecured. Fit the new driver's mat with the vehicle's intended retention system and check the pedal area before driving.

What should I do if the driver's mat will not stay clipped in?

Remove the loose mat and resolve the attachment or application problem before using it. Do not drive with a mat that can shift toward the pedals. Photograph its label, retention points, and installed position for support; avoid cutting or adding improvised fasteners.

Should I use a heat gun to flatten a new floor liner?

Do not improvise a heating method. Check the supplied directions or ask the seller about the specific deformation. Send a photo and the part label. If the driver's liner cannot sit securely with clear pedal space, keep it out of use while the issue is resolved.

Does an advertised no-drill installation mean no other changes are needed?

No. Read the preparation and compatibility conditions. An installation may require accessory removal or other work even when its mounts do not require drilling. The inspected OEDRO Tacoma cover page, for example, requires cargo-rack removal for the described setup.

Can I drive with a tonneau cover partly folded?

Only use positions expressly allowed and secured by the matching product instructions. Do not assume another cover's straps or props authorize the same position on yours. OEDRO's inspected Tacoma cover page prohibits driving partially open.

Can I use the shackles supplied with every aftermarket bumper for recovery?

No. A supplied ring is not proof of a rated recovery point. OEDRO's product 9456 warning states that its shackle is not for towing or recovery. Use only equipment and attachment points explicitly approved for the intended operation.

How should I clean OEDRO TPE floor liners?
