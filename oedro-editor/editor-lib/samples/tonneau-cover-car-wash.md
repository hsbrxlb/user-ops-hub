# Can You Take a Truck With a Tonneau Cover Through a Car Wash?

Sometimes, but check the instructions for your exact cover before entering an automatic wash. A touchless wash avoids brush contact, yet its water pressure and chemicals still matter. If you cannot confirm that the wash is suitable, choose a hand wash using the cleaning method specified for your cover.

For an OEDRO cover, use the matching manual or ask OEDRO support about the selected product. Another manufacturer's car-wash advice does not establish that an OEDRO cover can use the same wash, cleaner, or treatment.

## Which wash should you choose?

A wash with brushes or cloth rollers should be avoided unless your cover manufacturer expressly permits it. RealTruck, which owns several tonneau-cover brands, warns that brushes can catch edges and scratch surfaces. A rigid panel does not remove the risk to its finish, hinges, or seals.

A touchless automatic wash removes the brush-contact problem, but it is still a conditional choice. RealTruck notes that pressure and detergents can affect seals and finishes. Tyger's own FAQ recommends only touchless car washes for its covers and warns against wax treatments. That is Tyger's guidance for its products, rather than a rule for every truck bed cover.

A hand wash gives you control over the cleaner and where the water goes. Sawtooth recommends rinsing away dirt before using mild soap and a soft mitt or microfiber towel. Check your cover's care instructions before choosing the soap or adding a protectant. A self-service pressure wand also needs care; control of the wand alone does not make every spray setting suitable.

If you are still identifying your cover's construction, the [soft vs. hard tonneau cover guide](soft-vs-hard-tonneau-covers.html) explains the differences. Use the product label or order details to find the exact model before looking up its cleaning instructions.

## Check the cover before entering

Read the care section before paying for a wash package. Ask the operator whether the selected cycle uses brushes, spray wax, or other surface treatments. If the answer conflicts with your cover's instructions, choose a different wash.

Sawtooth's guidance calls for checking the perimeter, latches, clamps, and seals before washing. Look for a loose corner, lifted edge, torn material, or a latch that will not seat. Resolve those problems before using an automatic wash.

For a cover whose instructions allow the chosen wash, close and secure it as directed. Do not improvise a partly folded or rolled position to keep brushes off the top. If removal is required, follow the removal procedure before arriving at the wash.

Remove moisture-sensitive cargo beforehand. A cover that keeps out ordinary rain can still admit water during washing; Sawtooth specifically notes that pressurized water can reach gaps around the bed. An automatic wash is a poor place to find out whether your laptop bag will stay dry.

## What to check afterward

Park safely and inspect the cover before continuing your trip. Check for shifted seals, loose corners, and a change in latch engagement. Open the tailgate, check the bed for water, and dry accessible standing water.

If you find a new leak or a latch problem, record what changed and which wash cycle you used. The [tonneau cover troubleshooting guide](tonneau-cover-troubleshooting.html) explains what to document before adjusting hardware or contacting support. Keep the cover out of use if it cannot be secured according to its operating instructions.
