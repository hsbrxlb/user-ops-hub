Clean air isn't fresh air

Filter a glass of water a hundred times and you get a very well-filtered glass of the same water. A particle purifier does that to the air in your room. It draws room air through a filter, catches dust, pollen and smoke particles, and sends the same air back out. The air coming back is cleaner. It's the same air the room already had. Fresh air is a separate job. Outdoor air comes in and some room air goes out. In a shut room, everyone in it breathes out CO₂, and a particle filter doesn't remove it. With little outdoor air coming in, it stays in the room and builds. The last of the three pages puts a floor purifier and a FreshFlow in the same bedroom. The purifier takes particles out. FreshFlow is the white box installed in the window. It filters outdoor air as it brings it in. How high can CO₂ climb in a shut room?
